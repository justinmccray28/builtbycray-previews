Services

Everything your locs need, start to finish.

Whether you're just starting your journey or you've been growing for years, there's a service here for where you are now. Call to ask what's right for your hair.

01

Starter Locs

Begin your loc journey the right way, with a method chosen for your hair's texture, length and the look you're after.

02

Retwists & Maintenance

Regular upkeep that keeps new growth neat and your parts clean, so your locs stay full and healthy between visits.

03

Interlocking

A tight, lasting maintenance option for those who want their roots to hold longer between appointments.

04

Loc Styling

Updos, barrels, petals and twists for everyday wear, special events, photos or just because you deserve it.

05

Loc Repair

Thinning spots, weak points or locs that need some love. Bring them in and talk through options to restore them.

06

Wash & Scalp Care

A thorough cleanse and conditioning for your locs and scalp, to clear buildup and keep things fresh and comfortable.
