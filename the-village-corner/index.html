Our table

Old-world comfort, Stone Mountain hospitality.

Some meals are about more than the food. They're about slowing down, raising a glass and sharing a table with people you like.

That's the spirit of The Village Corner: German-style cooking that's hearty, honest and made to be enjoyed. Whether you're craving something you grew up with or trying German food for the first time, there's a seat waiting for you.

Find us on James B Rivers Memorial Drive, an easy trip from anywhere in metro Atlanta.

From the kitchen

The flavors of a German kitchen

The kind of classic, satisfying cooking German tables are known for.

Schnitzel

Goldbraun & knusprig

Golden, crisp cutlets: the dish that comes to mind when most people think of German comfort food.

Sausages & Wurst

Vom Grill

Savory sausages with the classic sides they belong with: mustard, kraut and something warm on the plate.

Pretzels & Breads

Frisch & warm

Soft, salty and made for sharing. The perfect start to the meal, or the perfect excuse for another round.

Hearty Sides

Spätzle, Kraut & mehr

Tender noodles, tangy cabbage and potatoes done the old-fashioned way: the sides that make a German plate.

Drinks

Zum Wohl

Something cold to go with something hearty. Ask about what's pouring when you visit.

Desserts

Kaffee & Kuchen

Strudel, cakes and sweet things in the German tradition. Save room; you'll be glad you did.
