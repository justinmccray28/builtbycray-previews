About the bar

A pub that feels like somebody's house.

Bob & Harriet's Home Bar is exactly what the name promises: a neighborhood spot on Hosea L Williams Drive where Kirkwood comes to unwind, catch up and stay a little longer than planned.

No velvet rope, no pretense. Just a good drink, something tasty to go with it and a room full of neighbors. Stop in after work, meet friends on the weekend, or grab food to take home.

Come as you are. Stay a while.
