How it works

From blank cloth to bragging rights.

No art background required. Just show up curious and ready to get a little fuzzy.

Pick a design

Choose something simple and bold, or bring your own idea to sketch onto the cloth.

Choose your yarn

Build a color palette that matches your room, your vibe, or your mood that day.

Tuft it

Get comfortable with the tufting gun and fill in your design loop by loop.

Finish & show off

Trim, shape, and finish your piece, then go find the perfect spot to show it off.
