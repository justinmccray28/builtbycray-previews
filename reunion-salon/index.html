Services

Everything your hair needs, under one roof.

Every visit starts with a conversation about your hair, your routine and what you want to feel when you leave. Call to talk through options and pricing.

01

Cuts & Shaping

Tailored cuts for every length and texture, from a simple refresh to a full change, shaped to grow out beautifully.

02

Color

All-over color, root touch-ups, gray blending and glosses, planned around your skin tone and how often you want to come in.

03

Highlights & Balayage

Soft, dimensional brightness, from face-framing pieces to sun-kissed blends that look natural as they grow.

04

Blowouts & Styling

Smooth, bouncy or sleek. A polished finish for a busy week, a night out, or just because.

Treatments

Conditioning and repair treatments that bring back softness and shine to hair that's been through a lot.

Special Occasions

Updos and event styling for weddings, celebrations and, yes, reunions. Call ahead to plan your look.
