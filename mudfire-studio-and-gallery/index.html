What happens here

One building, four ways to get muddy.

Whether you've never touched a wheel or you're looking for kiln space for your own practice, there's a place for you at Mudfire. Call and we'll point you to the right fit.

01

Classes & workshops

Hands-on time at the wheel and the table, for first-timers and returning potters alike. Ask about what's on the calendar.

02

Maker space

A shared studio for people who want room to work, the right tools on hand, and a community of makers around them.

03

Glaze & kiln firing

Where raw clay turns into finished work. Glazing and firing are part of the process, so your pieces come out ready to use.

04

The gallery

Handmade ceramics you can hold before you take them home. A good stop for a one-of-a-kind gift, or something for your own table.

Why Mudfire

Not a showroom. A working studio you can walk into.

Mudfire brings the whole life of a pot under one roof: the wheel, the glaze, the kiln and the gallery shelf. It's a local place to learn, make and buy, right off Laredo Drive.

  • Studio, gallery and maker space together

    Learn it, make it and see finished work, all in the same building.

  • Room for every skill level

    Curious beginners and longtime makers share the same space.

  • Rooted in Decatur

    An easy trip from anywhere in metro Atlanta, and a real neighborhood studio.

How it works

From a lump of clay to your shelf.

Getting started is simple. No experience needed, just a willingness to get a little messy.

01

Give us a call

Ring the studio to ask about classes, maker space or the gallery, and to check current hours.

02

Come make something

Wear clothes you don't mind getting muddy. Center, pull, shape, trim, then let it dry.

03

Glaze, fire, take it home

Your piece gets glazed and goes through the kiln, then comes back finished and ready to use.

Ready when you are

The wheel's waiting.

Questions about classes, studio space or something you spotted in the gallery? A quick call is the fastest way to get an answer.
