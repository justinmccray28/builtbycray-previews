What we do

Fresh, fluffy & feeling good.

From a quick tidy-up to a full spa day, every visit is about a clean, comfortable, happy pet. Call us to talk through what your pet needs.

Bath & brush

A warm wash, thorough rinse, blow-dry and brush-out that leaves coats soft and smelling clean.

Haircuts & trims

Full grooms, breed-style cuts, or a simple face, feet and tail tidy. Tell us the look you love.

Nails & paws

Nail trims and paw-pad tidying to keep every step comfortable on floors and sidewalks.

De-shedding care

Extra brushing for heavy shedders, so less fur ends up on your couch, car and clothes.

Cat grooming

Calm, patient grooming for cats too: baths, brush-outs and trims at a pace they’re comfortable with.

Pet boarding

Heading out of town? Your dog or cat gets a cozy place to stay while you’re away.

Boarding

Going away? They’ll be in good paws.

Whether it’s a weekend trip or a longer getaway, give us a call to check availability and talk through your pet’s routine.
