What we do

Everything your jewelry needs, under one small roof.

From a brand-new piece to a favorite ring that needs some love, every job gets the same careful attention.

01

Custom design

Start with an idea, a photo, or a rough sketch. We shape it into a one-of-a-kind piece that is unmistakably yours.

02

Engagement & bridal

Engagement rings, wedding bands, and matching sets, designed around your story and the way you live.

03

Jewelry repair

Broken chains, loose stones, worn prongs, and bent shanks, carefully repaired so your pieces are ready to wear again.

04

Ring sizing

Sized up or down for a comfortable, secure fit, with care taken to keep the look of the original.

05

Heirloom redesign

Give a family piece a second life. Reset stones or rework old gold into something you will actually wear every day.

06

Cleaning & check-ups

A professional clean and a close look at settings and clasps, so small issues get caught before stones go missing.

How custom works

From first sketch to finished piece.

Because the shop is owner-operated, you work with the same person from the first conversation to the final polish.

  1. Call or stop in

    Tell us what you have in mind. Bring inspiration photos, a stone, or a piece you want to rework.

  2. Design together

    We talk through metals, stones, style, and budget, and sketch out a direction you feel great about.

  3. Crafted with care

    Your piece is made with attention to every detail, and we keep you in the loop along the way.

  4. Pick up and wear it

    Come back to Clairemont Avenue, try it on, and take home something made just for you.
