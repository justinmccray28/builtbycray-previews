What a day can look like

Care that grows with your child

Every day is a mix of play, learning and plenty of good old-fashioned attention. Here's a taste of what childcare at Bundles of Joy is all about.

Daily childcare

A safe, nurturing space with caring grown-ups, so you can head to work knowing your little one is in good hands.

Early learning

Letters, numbers, colors and shapes, woven into stories, songs and hands-on activities that make learning fun.

Creative play

Art, music, building and pretend play that spark imagination and help kids build confidence and new skills.

Friends & social skills

Sharing, taking turns, using kind words. Kids learn how to get along and make friends along the way.
