Our story

A Little Five Points original.

Since 1992, Aurora has been pouring coffee on Moreland Avenue, a neighborhood spot for the record diggers, the artists, the students and everybody in between.

We're independent and proudly local. No chain playbook, just a coffeehouse that's been part of the neighborhood's rhythm for a long time. Come in for a quick cup on your way somewhere, or stay a while and soak in the L5P energy.
