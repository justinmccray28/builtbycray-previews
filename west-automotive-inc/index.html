01 What we work on

Everyday repairs, done right.

From a warning light that won’t go away to a squeal every time you stop, bring it by. Not sure what you need? Call and describe it. That’s what we’re here for.

SVC-01

Diagnostics

Check engine light on? We’ll find what’s behind it and explain the options in plain language.

SVC-02

Brakes

Squeaks, grinding, or a soft pedal. Pads, rotors, and brake system checks so you stop with confidence.

SVC-03

Oil & Fluids

Oil changes and fluid service to keep things running smooth between bigger visits.

SVC-04

Suspension & Steering

Clunks over bumps, pulling to one side, or a bouncy ride. We’ll track it down.

SVC-05

A/C & Heating

Georgia summers don’t wait. Get your A/C blowing cold and your heat ready for winter mornings.

SVC-06

Battery & Electrical

Slow starts, dim lights, or gremlins in the dash. Batteries, charging systems, and wiring issues.
